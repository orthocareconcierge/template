6 Week Schedule

Tap and hold a day to swap

Week 1

Hold and drag any two days to swap them

Day Type Focus

Upper Pull

Day A
Strength focus. Seated cable row, lat pulldown, TRX row, face pull, ER work. 3 to 4 sets of 8 to 12 reps. 60 to 90 second rest.
Day B
Endurance focus. Higher rep, lighter load. Band pull-apart, sidelying ER, Y-T-W, hammer curl. 3 sets of 15 to 20 reps. 30 to 45 second rest.
Day C
Functional and serratus focus. LT wall liftoff, serratus punch, wall clocks, wall slide with band. Motor pattern and scapular control emphasis.

Upper Push

Day A
Scapular plane strength. Cable lateral raise, D2 extension, half-kneeling cable press, tricep work. 3 sets of 8 to 12 reps.
Day B
Endurance and diagonal patterns. Seated DB press, cable low to high lift, band press, glute bridge with serratus punch. 3 sets of 15 reps.
Day C
Sling integration. D2 flexion, cable chop, serratus punch standing, wall clocks progression. Functional movement emphasis.

Lower Strength

Day A
Compound strength. Bilateral leg press, hip thrust with band, RDL, single leg press, hamstring curl, hip ABD machine, step up.
Day B
Posterior chain and hip ER emphasis. Cable pull-through, standing clam, lateral band walk, hip ADD machine, LAQ on leg extension machine.
Day C
Hip stability and single leg focus. Kickstand SL RDL, TRX squat, wall sit with ball squeeze, standing cable hip ER, step up progression.

Lower Endurance

Day A
Tempo and unilateral work. Goblet squat 4-3-1 tempo, luggage squat, split squat with band, kickstand RDL, TRX lateral lunge. 3 sets of 12 to 20 reps.
Day B
Functional and hip flexor focus. Reverse lunge from step, cable pull-through, eccentric hip flexor lowering, prone hamstring curl, wall sit. 3 sets of 15 reps.
Day C
Balance and hip stability. Star excursion, lateral hurdle, TRX SL squat assist, 4 way kicks, standing cable hip ER. Proprioceptive emphasis.

Core and Balance

Day A
Stability and anti-movement. Deadbug, Pallof press, bear crawl hold, plank variations, bird dog, foam roller thoracic extension.
Day B
Trunk slings. Glute bridge with serratus punch, cable chop and lift, Pallof with rotation, eccentric hip flexion with rotation, thread the needle.
Day C
Balance and proprioception. SL foam balance with ball circles, BOSU black side up, star excursion, reactive ball toss, tandem walk, open book.
Guiding Principles

Schedule a check-in with Kelly every other week to review progress, adjust loads, and progress exercises. Do not self-progress. If a session feels off, reduce load and focus on form. Your body will tell you when something is wrong. Learn to listen to it.

Daily Protocol

Home rehab program

Each day, choose 2 to 3 upper extremity and 2 to 3 lower extremity exercises to complete. Rotate through them so you are hitting all exercises across the week. Check each one off as you complete it. When you finish all exercises in a section, that section resets for the next round.
